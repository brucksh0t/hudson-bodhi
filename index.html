Our Story

Bodhi is Sanskrit for “awakening.”

Melinda Macchiaroli began her journey into holistic wellness in 1995, studying massage therapy, Reiki, yoga and shamanic healing traditions. While studying in Dharamsala, India, she received a Bodhicitta initiation from His Holiness the Dalai Lama, and the word Bodhi became the foundation for her vision.

She founded Bodhi in 2005. In 2011, Melinda and her husband TJ purchased the historic Bodhi building and lovingly restored it. With its soaring ceilings, beautiful architecture and peaceful atmosphere, the building itself is part of the Bodhi experience.

Melinda is also the creator of SÖL Skin Ritual, the plant-based skincare and body collection used throughout our treatments.

“Our intention is the same: to create a space where you feel supported, nourished, and inspired to return to yourself.”
